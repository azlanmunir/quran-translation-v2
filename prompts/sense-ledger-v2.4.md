# Sense Ledger v2.4

This is a contextual sense ledger, not a substitution table. The structured source
of truth is `evidence/sense-ledger-v2.4.json`; this file is its compact model-facing
companion.

## Rules

1. The basmala is the only invariant wording in this ledger:
   **"In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate."**
2. Select a sense from construction, syntax, immediate context, and concordance.
   A root never determines the translation by itself.
3. A listed rendering is a contextual default, not a command to use the same English
   at every occurrence.
4. If no recorded sense fits, translate the passage rather than forcing the ledger;
   add a review flag and preserve the uncertainty.
5. Evidence summaries constrain decisions but are not reader notes. Do not turn them
   into claims about etymology, history, or what every first hearer consciously felt.

Confidence: **[A]** strong; **[B]** supported but review-sensitive; **[C]** contested.

## Prohibited global substitutions

- Do not globally render *āmana* as "entrust oneself" or *muʾmin* as "one who
  entrusts himself."
- Do not globally render *dīn* as "way of allegiance," "system," or "debt."
- Do not use "fornication" as the sole rendering of *zinā*; in current English it
  can wrongly exclude adultery.
- Do not force "the Writ," "written for you," "prayer-rite," "Gehenna," "the
  Recitation," or "this nearer life" into contexts where plain English is more
  accurate.
- Do not add universal scope: an intensive form such as *ghafūr* does not by itself
  mean "all-forgiving."
- Do not drop lexical content from rare terms: *al-mawʾūdah* includes live burial,
  and *al-ʿihn* includes dyed or colored wool.
- Do not silently collapse *kursiyy* into *ʿarsh*, or the gender-scope question in
  legal *ikhwah*; preserve or note the distinction.
