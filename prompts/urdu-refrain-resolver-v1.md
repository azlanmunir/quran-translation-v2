# Urdu Refrain Resolver v1

For each group, the Arabic ayah is byte-identical at every listed reference but the
draft pipeline produced more than one Urdu rendering. Choose exactly one of the
supplied Urdu options as the canonical rendering for every occurrence.

Judge fidelity, legal and rhetorical force, clarity aloud, and consistency with the
Urdu ledger. Do not write a new rendering and do not combine options. Return every
group in the supplied order under the registered JSON contract.
